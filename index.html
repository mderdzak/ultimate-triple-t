Nine boards, one winner. Win three small boards in a row to take the game.

How to play
  • The board is a 3 × 3 grid of nine small tic tac toe boards.
  • The square you play in sends your opponent to the matching small board. Play the top-right square and they must play in the top-right board.
  • If you're sent to a board that's already won or full, you may play in any open board.
  • Win a small board with three in a row and it counts as your square on the big board.
  • Win three small boards in a row to win the game. If no moves are left, it's a draw.
